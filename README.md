## 🌟 Executive Overview

**CXPulse** is an award-quality, executive-grade AI customer intelligence and retention platform. Built for modern fast-moving SaaS enterprises, CXPulse replaces reactive ticketing with autonomous telemetry that understands customer sentiment, dynamically prioritizes urgent issues by revenue risk, and equips support teams with real-time AI response composers and orbital health maps.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. 🪐 CX Radar™ (Signature Feature)
* **Orbital Customer Health Topology**: Maps customer sentiment (orbital angle) against churn risk (distance from center).
* **Segment Visualizer**: Filter accounts across **Happy**, **Satisfied**, **Neutral**, **At Risk**, and **Critical**.
* **Interactive Targeting Reticle**: Hover over nodes to inspect company health, ARR, open issues, and click directly into 360° account intelligence.
* **Multi-Parameter Controls**: Filter by sentiment range, churn probability, ARR tier (Enterprise/Mid-Market/SMB), and ticket count.

### 2. ⚡ Autonomous AI Priority Queue ("Needs Attention")
* Prioritizes tickets dynamically by **Customer Lifetime Value (ARR)** and **Churn Probability** rather than standard first-in-first-out queues.
* Immediate visibility into intent, latent emotional distress, and waiting time.

### 3. 🎯 AI Customer Intelligence (Signature Component)
* **Deep Latent Analysis**: Extracts Intent, Sentiment, Emotion, Priority, and Churn Risk score in under 15ms.
* **Structured Causality ("Why this risk?")**: Breaks down specific contributing factors (e.g. unresolved complaints, delayed refunds, negative call sentiment, satisfaction dips).
* **Next Best Action Sequence**: Numbered, actionable operational recommendations for immediate retention.

### 4. ✍️ AI Response Composer
* Context-aware response generation pre-loaded with customer history and technical incident context.
* **One-Click Tone Modulators**:
  * `[↻ Regenerate]` — Synthesizes fresh alternatives.
  * `[Shorter]` — Condensed executive briefing.
  * `[Empathetic]` — Emotionally calibrated for high-frustration accounts.
  * `[Professional]` — Crisp formal communication.
* **Working Action Triggers**: `[Save Draft]`, `[Send Response]`, and `[Escalate]`.

### 5. 📈 Interactive Sentiment Analytics
* High-DPI Canvas spline visualization rendering positive, neutral, and negative sentiment distribution.
* Dynamic time windows (`24h`, `7d`, `30d`) with precision hover tooltips.

### 6. 🔄 Progressive AI Loading Experience
* Demonstrates live inference steps when analyzing conversations:
  1. *Understanding customer intent...*
  2. *Detecting sentiment...*
  3. *Evaluating customer risk...*
  4. *Generating recommended response...*

### 7. 🔍 Global Instant Search (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`)
* Instant keyboard-accessible modal searching across all customer names, companies, ARR figures, and churn status.

### 8. 🌓 Dark-First & Light Mode
* Sophisticated dark theme with ambient glassmorphism and tailored HSL color tokens.
* One-click toggle in the top bar transforms the interface into a clean, modern light mode.

* **Core**: Modern Semantic HTML5, CSS3, ES6+ JavaScript.
* **Visual Engine**: Custom Canvas 2D engine for smooth cubic Bezier charts and high-FPS radar orbitals.
* **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans & JetBrains Mono.
* **Zero External Build Step**: Instantaneous sub-100ms load time with zero npm bloat or compilation delay.
